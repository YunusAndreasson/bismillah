# Notes on *uṣūl al-fiqh*

## Emphasis

The four sources are the Qurʾān, the sunna, ijmāʿ and qiyās. Terms are italicised with stars, as in *sharīʿa* and *maṣlaḥa*, or with underscores, as in _sunna_, _ijmāʿ_, _fatwā_ and _taqlīd_. Bold works too: **al-Shāfiʿī** and __Ibn Ḥanbal__.

A term ending in a vowel inside emphasis is common: _sharīʿa_, *ʿāda*, _hidāya_, _shūrā_, *fiqh al-aqalliyyāt* and _ʿilla_.
