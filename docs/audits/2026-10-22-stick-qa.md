# Exploratory QA on the stick (2026-10-22)

An exploratory pass over the editor as a writer meets it on the bismillah stick, after OS-5. The debug image ran in QEMU with OVMF and Secure Boot on (`os/scripts/vm.ts`), driven only by keystrokes over QMP, with the screen read from screendumps and the stick's files and logs from the debug image's root console. Each finding was then reproduced in the real app on the host (tauri-driver, `--appliance` with a stand-in helper where the stick mattered), fixed, and covered by a test that fails without the fix.

What was used: setup, New file through the native dialog, typing and paragraphs, the library (docked, focus, rename, new folder), quick open, the palette, Find and Replace, Go to line, spelling suggestions, Zuhd mode, typewriter scrolling, *Always show Markdown*, text size, themes, Settings (every section, This stick's layouts and the restart they cause), Arabic and mixed lines on an Arabic layout, smart punctuation, copy and paste, undo, a 187 kB document of 1 500 sections, the guide, About, Lock with a wrong and a right passphrase, and Power off with Ctrl+Q straight after typing.

## Seen and left

- **GTK's "Replace?" question doesn't show under cage.** Typing an existing name in New file and pressing Enter seems to do nothing; a second Enter answers the hidden question, and bismillah then refuses: "Couldn't create the file — a file with that name already exists." Nothing is lost. rfd turns GTK's overwrite confirmation on unconditionally, so removing the question needs a patched rfd, or a look at how cage stacks GTK's nested dialogs.
- **The theme key cycles System, Light, Dark.** Where the system is dark, one press from Dark to System changes nothing visible. As designed, but it reads as a dead key.
- **cage's pointer** still shows over the lock screen and the text until the writer types (os/PLAN.md, OS-5).
- **Grammar** didn't mark "it were good." Harper's rules, not a defect of the app.
- **Rename and Move to trash aren't offered by the palette** for a focused library row: opening the palette moves focus away from the row. F2 and Delete in the library work.

## Checked and sound

The New file dialog keeps to the library; text survives Power off straight after typing; Arabic typed through the Arabic layout (Alt+Shift) is saved with its marks and laid out right to left beside English; smart punctuation gives curly quotes, an en dash and an ellipsis; applying new layouts restarts the session and comes back to the same document; a wrong passphrase on the lock clears the field and keeps focus there; the caret, Go to line and paging stay quick in a 1 500-section document.

## Power and the fan

Asked afterwards for quick wins that keep everything working and keep the laptop quiet.

- **Idle is already quiet.** 61 s with the text open and the caret settled, in the VM: the whole system busy 0.17% of the time (bismillah 60 ms, WebKit 40 ms, cage and the kernel 30 ms), about 11 timer interrupts per CPU per second. The caret stops blinking after ten seconds; the clock (5 s) and the battery (30 s) are the app's only timers, and every helper loop waits on a socket or a signal. On the desktop, `pnpm perf` measured the same (docs/audits/2026-10-20-resource-use.md).
- **The NVIDIA card in the Dell sleeps.** Its wake-ups in the OS-0 report came from the report's own probe (os/spikes/boot/DELL-CHECK.md).
- **Changed: the CPU prefers saving power** (`balance_power`, tmpfiles.d/bismillah.conf). With no power daemon, intel_pstate keeps the firmware's preference, usually `balance_performance`, which boosts to full turbo for every small burst of work: the chip heats and the fan ramps. The CPU still speeds up for sustained work. The setting lives in the CPU and is gone at power off. The VM has no such driver, so the effect is the Dell's to judge: the fan while writing, and the time from *Unlock* to the text (2.0 s in the VM; the KDF may take a little longer).
- **Changed: no lockup detectors** (`nowatchdog`), which woke every CPU every few seconds.
- **Not done: Dell's thermal modes** (*Quiet*, *Cool*). They would quiet the fan most, but the firmware keeps the choice, and the stick leaves the laptop untouched.
